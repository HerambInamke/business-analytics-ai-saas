# Business Analytics & Market Research for AI/SaaS Products

## Overview
This repository demonstrates structured business analytics, market research,
and data-driven decision support for AI and SaaS products.

The work simulates a founder-level analytics role, focusing on:
- Competitor benchmarking
- Market gap identification
- Product–market fit analysis
- Customer feedback evaluation
- KPI and funnel tracking
- Lightweight Python automation

## Objectives
- Identify competitive positioning in AI/SaaS markets
- Support go-to-market and product decisions using data
- Convert qualitative feedback into actionable insights
- Design dashboards for leadership decision-making

## Tools Used
- Excel / CSV (data analysis & benchmarking)
- Power BI (dashboard design – conceptual)
- Python (automation & data preprocessing)
- Markdown (structured reporting)

## Key Deliverables
- Competitor & pricing analysis
- SWOT and market gap insights
- Customer feedback synthesis
- KPI & funnel framework
- Automation scripts for research efficiency
