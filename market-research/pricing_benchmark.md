# Pricing Benchmark – AI/SaaS Market

## Pricing Models Observed
- Freemium
- Per-seat subscription
- Usage-based pricing

## Sample Pricing Table
| Product | Entry Plan | Pro Plan | Enterprise |
|---------|-----------|----------|------------|
| Tool A  | $29/mo    | $79/mo   | Custom     |
| Tool B  | $49/mo    | $99/mo   | Custom     |
| Tool C  | $19/mo    | $59/mo   | $199/mo    |

## Insights
- Entry pricing below $30 increases trial adoption
- Usage-based pricing aligns better with AI compute costs
- Enterprise plans often delay conversions due to sales friction

## Recommendation
Adopt a **low-friction starter tier + usage-based scaling**
