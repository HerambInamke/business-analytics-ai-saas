# SWOT Analysis – AI Product Ecosystem

## Strengths
- AI-driven automation
- Flexible architecture
- Faster iteration cycles

## Weaknesses
- Low brand awareness
- Limited early user feedback
- Resource constraints

## Opportunities
- Growing demand for AI productivity tools
- Underserved SMB market
- Founder-led brand storytelling

## Threats
- Highly funded competitors
- Rapid AI commoditization
- Customer trust & data privacy concerns
